# TechSafeAI Website - Standard Operating Procedure v1.0

## SECTION 1: WEBSITE ACCESS AND USER AUTHENTICATION PROCEDURES

Access the TechSafeAI platform at the designated URL provided by your system administrator. New users must complete initial registration using a valid company email address. Upon registration, you will receive a verification email within 5 minutes. Click the verification link to activate your account.

Login using your registered email and password. If you encounter login issues, use the "Forgot Password" function to reset credentials. All password reset requests are logged for security purposes.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enabled by default for HSE Officer and Administrator roles. Upon successful password entry, enter the 6-digit code sent to your registered mobile device. MFA codes expire after 10 minutes.

Session timeout occurs after 30 minutes of inactivity. Save your work regularly to prevent data loss. If your session expires during data entry, unsaved information will be lost.

## SECTION 2: NAVIGATION STRUCTURE AND MAIN PORTAL PAGES

After login, you will land on the Dashboard page. The Dashboard displays active incidents, pending tasks, recent reports, and compliance alerts specific to your role and location.

Primary navigation menu is located on the left sidebar and includes:
- Dashboard (home)
- New Incident (report creation)
- Incident Register (all records)
- Reports (analytics and exports)
- Settings (user preferences)

Top navigation bar contains notification bell, help resources, and user profile menu. Click your profile icon to access account settings or logout.

Use breadcrumb navigation at the top of each page to track your location within the system and quickly return to previous screens.

## SECTION 3: MODULE CATALOGUE AND PRODUCT PRESENTATION

TechSafeAI offers industry-specific compliance modules tailored to operational requirements. The platform currently supports Construction, Mining, Manufacturing, and Oil & Gas sectors. Each module contains jurisdiction-specific regulatory frameworks and reporting templates.

To view available modules, navigate to Settings, then select Module Catalogue from the left menu. The catalogue displays all modules available to your organization based on your subscription tier and geographic location.

Each module listing shows:
- Industry sector and jurisdiction coverage
- Included incident types and workflows
- Regulatory standards addressed
- Required user roles for access
- Implementation status (Active, Pending, or Trial)

Active modules appear in your New Incident workflow dropdown menu. Trial modules are accessible for 30 days with full functionality but include watermarked exports. Pending modules require administrator activation before use.

To request additional modules, click Request Module Access and complete the justification form. Requests are reviewed by your organization administrator within 2 business days. Approved requests may require additional licensing fees.

Module-specific documentation is accessible by clicking the information icon next to each module name. This documentation includes workflow diagrams, field definitions, compliance mapping tables, and sample completed reports.

When presenting module capabilities to stakeholders, use the Export Module Summary function to generate a PDF overview containing feature lists, compliance coverage, and implementation requirements. This summary does not require active module subscription to generate.

## SECTION 4: PILOTS AND IMPLEMENTATION

TechSafeAI pilot programs are designed to validate system functionality within your operational environment before full deployment. Pilots are volume-gated to control scope and ensure quality data collection during evaluation.

Pilot scope is limited to a maximum of 50 incident reports per organization. This threshold ensures adequate testing of core workflows while maintaining manageable data review during the evaluation phase. Once the 50-incident limit is reached, the system enters read-only mode until full implementation is approved.

Risk control measures during pilot phase include:
- Restricted user access to designated pilot team members only
- Automatic data backup every 24 hours
- Audit trail activation for all user actions
- Sandbox environment separation from production systems
- Administrator approval required for all user invitations

Governance structure requires designation of a Pilot Lead who serves as primary contact for technical support and feedback collection. The Pilot Lead receives weekly usage reports and is responsible for coordinating user training sessions.

Onboarding flow for pilot participants:
1. Pilot Lead submits participant list to TechSafeAI administrator
2. System generates unique invitation links for each participant
3. Participants complete registration and mandatory orientation module
4. Pilot Lead assigns initial test incident scenarios
5. Participants complete minimum 3 practice incidents before live data entry
6. Weekly check-in meetings scheduled for first 4 weeks

Upon successful pilot completion, the Pilot Lead submits evaluation report documenting system performance, user feedback, and readiness recommendation. Full implementation approval requires sign-off from both Pilot Lead and organizational HSE Manager.

## SECTION 5: COMPLIANCE MAPPING

TechSafeAI provides jurisdiction-specific compliance mapping to assist organizations in meeting regulatory reporting obligations. The system maps incident data fields to corresponding legislative requirements based on your selected industry sector and geographic location.

Compliance mapping operates as decision support only. The platform does not provide legal advice and should not be relied upon as a substitute for professional legal counsel. Organizations remain solely responsible for ensuring compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

When creating a new incident report, the system automatically identifies relevant regulatory frameworks based on incident classification, severity level, and location data. Mandatory fields required by legislation are marked with red asterisks and cannot be bypassed.

The compliance indicator panel displays real-time alignment status showing:
- Regulatory frameworks triggered by current incident parameters
- Completion percentage for each framework's requirements
- Missing data elements preventing compliance certification
- Recommended actions to achieve full regulatory alignment

Audit readiness features include automatic generation of compliance summary reports that cross-reference incident data against legislative requirements. These reports identify which regulatory obligations have been satisfied and highlight any gaps requiring attention.

For multi-jurisdiction operations, the system applies the most stringent requirements when overlapping regulations exist. Users receive notification when incident parameters trigger additional compliance obligations beyond initial classification.

All compliance mapping logic is updated quarterly to reflect legislative amendments and regulatory guidance updates. System administrators receive change notifications 30 days prior to compliance rule updates taking effect.

Organizations should conduct independent legal review of all compliance outputs before relying on system-generated assessments for regulatory submissions or legal proceedings.

## SECTION 6: CONTACT AND ENQUIRY HANDLING

All enquiries are submitted through the Contact Form on the TechSafeAI website. The form captures organization name, industry sector, jurisdiction, contact details, and enquiry type. Enquiry types include General Information, Pilot Request, Technical Support, and Module Consultation.

Upon form submission, you will receive an automated acknowledgment email within 5 minutes confirming receipt and providing a unique enquiry reference number. This reference number should be quoted in all subsequent correspondence.

Pilot requests undergo qualification assessment to ensure alignment with platform capabilities and organizational readiness. The qualification process evaluates:
- Industry sector compatibility with available modules
- Estimated incident volume and reporting frequency
- Current compliance management systems in use
- Number of anticipated users and role distribution
- Implementation timeline expectations

Initial response to pilot requests is provided within 2 business days. Qualified requests proceed to discovery call scheduling where detailed requirements are discussed. Non-qualified requests receive explanation of current platform limitations and alternative solutions where applicable.

General information enquiries receive response within 1 business day. Technical support requests for existing users are prioritized and addressed within 4 business hours during standard business hours.

All enquiry communications are logged in the customer relationship management system. Follow-up actions are tracked and escalated if response timelines are not met. Users can check enquiry status by referencing their unique enquiry number through the website portal.
