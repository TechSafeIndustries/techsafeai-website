Compliance Mapping

Jurisdiction-specific decision support for regulatory reporting obligations

TechSafeAI provides jurisdiction-specific compliance mapping to assist organisations in meeting regulatory reporting obligations. The system maps incident data fields to corresponding legislative requirements based on your selected industry sector and geographic location.

⚠️ Legal Disclaimer

Compliance mapping operates as decision support only. The platform does not provide legal advice and should not be relied upon as a substitute for professional legal counsel.

Automated Framework Identification

When creating a new incident report, the system automatically identifies relevant regulatory frameworks based on incident classification, severity level, and location data. Mandatory fields required by legislation are marked with red asterisks and cannot be bypassed.
